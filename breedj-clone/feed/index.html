The real risk: outsourcing without a clear framework

The main risk is not outsourcing abroad. The real risk is doing it without a sufficiently clear contractual, administrative and HR framework.

Many companies start with a very simple approach: they find a freelancer, exchange a few messages, agree on a rate and pay by bank transfer.

This can work for a one-off assignment. But as soon as the collaboration becomes regular, long-term or strategic, the risks increase.

The questions then become more sensitive:

  • What is the real status of the collaborator?
  • Is the contract appropriate?
  • Who manages local compliance?
  • What happens in the event of a dispute?
  • How are data and access secured?
  • How can the collaboration be ended properly?
  • Who carries the administrative responsibility?

For a French SME, these questions are not secondary. They can have legal, financial and operational consequences.

That is why choosing the right framework is just as important as choosing the right profile.

Common objections before getting started

It is normal to have doubts before outsourcing a first position. For many SMEs, this is a new way of working.

“What if the quality is not there?”

Quality depends first on the framework. A good profile with a poor brief can fail. A properly selected profile, well onboarded and managed with clear objectives, can deliver excellent results.

Breedj helps precisely with defining the need, preselecting profiles and structuring the onboarding.

“How do we manage cultural or time zone differences?”

The countries covered by Breedj often offer good time zone compatibility with France. But the most important factor remains organization: regular check-ins, simple objectives, shared tools, written instructions and clear communication.

Remote work functions well when expectations are explicit.

“What happens if the collaboration does not work?”

A clear framework makes it possible to anticipate difficult situations: trial period, replacement, end of assignment, termination, responsibilities, deadlines and exit conditions.

The goal is to avoid vague arrangements that become problematic at the first disagreement.

“Is it ethical to outsource at a lower cost?”

Yes, provided that the collaboration is legal, declared, transparent and fair locally.

Breedj’s model is not based on a dumping logic. It aims to create a long-term, compliant and professional collaboration between French companies and qualified talents.

“What will my accountant say?”

Your accountant will mainly want to understand the contract, the invoice, the provider’s status, the legal framework and the compliance of the model.

With a structured solution, these elements are easier to explain, document and analyze.
