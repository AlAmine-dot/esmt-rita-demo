Talk to an expert

In a context of evolving work models and increasing openness to flexible workforce structures, understanding international salary levels has become essential. A salary benchmark in Kenya allows companies to accurately assess costs, identify opportunities, and build high-performing remote teams.

Kenya is now emerging as a strategic hub for digital roles, outsourced services, and support functions. With a skilled, English-speaking, and tech-oriented workforce, the country is attracting more and more companies seeking competent talent at controlled costs.

This guide helps you better understand the market, salary levels, operational advantages, and compliant ways to collaborate.

Understanding the Job Market in Kenya

Kenya is one of the most dynamic economies in East Africa. Its capital, Nairobi, is often considered a major tech innovation hub, attracting startups, multinational companies, and skilled professionals.

The job market is characterized by:

  • Strong growth in the digital sector
  • A young, educated, and connected workforce
  • Excellent English proficiency
  • A professional culture aligned with international standards

Most in-demand roles include:

  • Web and software developers
  • Customer support agents
  • Digital marketing specialists
  • Accountants and financial analysts
  • Data analysts

Salary Levels in Kenya: What to Expect

Salaries vary depending on:

  • Experience level
  • Area of expertise
  • Type of collaboration (freelance, payroll, outsourcing)
  • Exposure to international companies

General Trends

  • Junior profiles are highly affordable
  • Mid-level profiles offer excellent value for money
  • Senior profiles, especially in IT, can approach international standards

Key Considerations

  • Local benefits (insurance, social contributions)
  • Differences between local and remote work markets
  • Global competition for tech talent

FAQ – Salary Benchmark in Kenya & Remote Hiring

1. What is a salary benchmark in Kenya?

A salary benchmark in Kenya is a tool that helps companies understand average compensation levels across different roles and industries. It allows businesses to evaluate costs, compare markets, and build competitive remote teams.

2. Why is Kenya an attractive market for remote talent?

Kenya offers a skilled, English-speaking workforce with strong expertise in digital, support, and business functions. Combined with competitive salary levels and a growing tech ecosystem, it makes the country highly attractive for international companies.

3. What types of roles are commonly outsourced to Kenya?

Companies typically hire Kenyan talent for roles such as web and software development, customer support, digital marketing, accounting, and data analysis.

4. Are Kenyan professionals easy to work with remotely?

Yes. Kenyan professionals are experienced in working with international companies and are familiar with digital tools, remote collaboration, and global work standards.

5. What are the main advantages of hiring in Kenya?

The key advantages include access to qualified talent, cost optimization, organizational flexibility, and strong English communication skills.

6. What challenges should companies anticipate?

Companies may face challenges related to legal compliance, payroll management, contracts, and administrative processes. Structuring remote teams also requires clear processes and organization.

7. How does Breedj help companies hire in Kenya?

Breedj provides Employer of Record (EOR) solutions, payroll management, and administrative support. It allows companies to hire and manage talent in Kenya without setting up a local entity, ensuring full legal and operational compliance.
